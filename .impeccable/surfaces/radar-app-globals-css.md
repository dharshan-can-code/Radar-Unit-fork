## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a survey sheet of water that cannot be seen. It refuses the dark space dashboard with a neon accent and the white-and-violet product page with a soft gradient.

OWN-WORLD: A topographic map sheet. Map-paper ground, neutral black ink, hydrography blue for every action, contour brown for the drawn background and index lines, a pale woodland tint for quiet fields, photorevision purple for calculated values. Panels are legend boxes: flat, one hairline, tight corners, no shadow. Cyan and amber belong to the water data only.

STORY: The visitor reads one explained result, chooses a place, and reads its map the way a printed sheet is read: the answer, then the legend, then the evidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline and two actions on plain paper; right, the radar plate in a neat-line frame. Behind both, contour lines closing around the plate, with index contours heavier, in place of any gradient.
